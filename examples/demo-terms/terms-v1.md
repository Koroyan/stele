# Northwind Shop Terms of Service

## 1. Orders
When you place an order you make an offer to buy the items in your cart. We confirm it by email.
We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock; you are then refunded in full.

## 2. Delivery
We ship within 3 business days. Delivery times shown at checkout are estimates.

## 3. Returns and refunds
Refund policy: 30 days. You may return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Return shipping is free.

## 4. Payment
Prices include VAT. You are charged when your order ships.
